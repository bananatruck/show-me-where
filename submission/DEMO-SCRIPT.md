# Demo script: Show Me Where (about 2 minutes 40 seconds)

Record the screen at 1440 wide in the light theme.
Narrate in your own words; the lines below are a guide, one sentence per line.
Everything shown in the sample run is a synthetic, hand-written example, and the narration must say so.
If a real recorded run replaces the sample before recording, change the 0:20 line to name the model and date.

## 0:00 to 0:20, the problem

Show the paste screen with the sample paragraph.
"When I write a lit review, I cite papers I skimmed weeks ago."
"Numbers drift, hedges disappear, and AI writing tools make it worse."
"Show Me Where checks each cited sentence against the source, and always shows me the sentence it relied on."

## 0:20 to 0:40, the sample run

Point at the label above the text box.
"This is the sample run. The paragraph, the sources and the model's answers are hand-written to show every outcome, and nothing leaves the browser."
Click "Find claims".

## 0:40 to 1:00, claims and sources

Scroll through step 2.
"It reads APA citations, including two sources in one bracket and a narrative citation."
"The uncited sentence is listed, not silently dropped."
Click "Check claims".

## 1:00 to 1:25, a supported and a partly supported claim

Show the first two results.
"Here is the sentence from the source, highlighted between the sentences around it."
"The grade says 'in the model's reading', because a real sentence can still be the wrong sentence."
"The second claim says 18 percent; the source says 8, and the app flags the number without trusting the model."

## 1:25 to 1:50, the discarded grade

Scroll to the third result.
"Here the model said supported, but its quote is not in the source."
"The app threw that grade away and says cannot tell."

## 1:50 to 2:10, sources that disagree, and a missing source

Show the caffeine claim, then the last claim.
"Two sources: one backs the claim and one contradicts it, so the app says sources disagree instead of picking the happy one."
"The last source has no text, so it is marked not reachable; in a live run I would paste it."

## 2:10 to 2:30, live mode and privacy

Switch to "Live with your key".
"In a live run, DOIs go to Europe PMC for open-access full text, then OpenAlex for the abstract, and each claim goes to a free OpenRouter model under my own key."
"The key stays in my browser, and the summary lists every service that was called."

## 2:30 to 2:40, close

Scroll to the summary panel.
"The app checks that every quote is really in the source. Whether it backs the claim is for me to read, and now I know exactly where to look."
